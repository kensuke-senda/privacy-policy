Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

This policy applies to all apps published by Kensuke Senda:

  • The Decision Coin
  • One Arcana

Data Collection

These apps do not collect, store, or share any personal data.

How the Apps Work

Each app runs entirely on your Apple Watch. No data is transmitted to external servers. Any settings you choose are stored only on your device and are removed when you delete the app.

Third Parties

No user data is collected, tracked, or shared with any third parties. These apps contain no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs.

Notifications

One Arcana can show a daily reminder if you turn it on. Notifications are scheduled locally on your Apple Watch. Nothing is sent to or through any server. You can turn them off at any time in the app.

Children's Privacy

These apps do not collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
